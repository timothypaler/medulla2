## Use and visual review

All nine listed originals were downloaded at their source resolution. Raster integrity checks passed. The two selected SVGs rendered successfully with Inkscape; the nine-figure contact sheet was visually inspected. The SVG reading files have no script, foreignObject or event-handler attributes. Preserve the original labels and panel/view captions.

The CC BY and CC BY-SA originals require attribution and the relevant license link. If adapted, state the changes; share-alike requirements apply to adaptations of the SA figures. The app code and other assets are not relicensed by this collection notice.

The 2013 OpenStax originals here have per-file CC BY 3.0 grants recorded by Commons. This does not assert that every file in a later OpenStax edition has the same license.

## Scope limits

### emb-early-neural
- **Neural plate, folds and neural crest** — Shows neural-fold convergence, tube formation and neural-crest separation. It does not show the complete timeline of embryonic folding, gastrulation or secondary neurulation.
### emb-msk-head
- **Pharyngeal arch arteries, pouches and cranial nerves** — Shows changing spatial relationships of arch arteries, pharyngeal pouches and cranial nerves. It is not a complete arch-derivative table or a dissection view.
- **Limb-bud anterior–posterior patterning** — Illustrates a classic experimental Shh patterning model and descendants of Shh-expressing cells. It is not a human gestational-age chart or a complete explanation of limb formation.
### emb-thoracoabdominal
- **Heart-tube development and chamber partitioning** — Shows heart-tube fusion, looping and simplified chamber partitioning. It does not depict every septal component, congenital variation or real embryonic specimen.
- **Intestinal herniation and morphogenesis** — Contrasts the conventional rotation description with a research-based hierarchical-looping account. The panels use different viewpoints and should retain the original figure caption and source context.
### emb-urogenital-applied
- **Pronephros, mesonephros and metanephric induction** — Shows transient nephric structures, ureteric budding and metanephric mesenchyme. It is not a human week-by-week staging chart, mature nephron atlas or congenital-anomaly diagnostic figure.
- **External genital development and homology** — An archival anatomical illustration of external genital differentiation. Original terminology is historical; use a current course atlas for preferred terminology, normal variation and precise developmental timing.
### emb-placenta-fetal
- **Placental organization and the maternal–fetal interface** — Shows placental villi, membranes, cord vessels and the maternal blood space. It is a simplified diagram, not placental histology or a normal-variation atlas.
- **Fetal circulation and the three shunts** — Shows fetal vascular routes and the ductus venosus, foramen ovale and ductus arteriosus. Colors simplify mixing and oxygenation; the figure is not a hemodynamic simulation.

## Excluded or optional candidates

- The animated fetal-circulation SVG marked as inaccurate/misleading on Commons was excluded.
- placenta-fetal-membranes.svg is a staged public-domain historical reference, not part of the nine preferred figure set.
- external-genital-development-color.png is a staged CC0 self-published candidate with mixed source references. It was inspected but is not in the preferred figure set pending stronger anatomical/source review. The historical Gray figure is retained instead.
