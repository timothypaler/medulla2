# Anatomy artwork provenance

The source SVGs are retained for traceability. Adaptations remove labels/arrows, extract and map individual bones, fit them into a common view, and replace source colors with interactive educational X-ray styling. These are illustrations, not patient radiographs. No medical certification or specialist review is claimed.

## Hand

`hand-source.svg` and `app/hand-art.json`: Mariana Ruiz Villarreal (LadyofHats), with retouching by Nyks and Bibi Saint-Pol and the no-text adaptation by was_a_bee. Public domain.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Scheme_human_hand_bones_-_no_text.svg

All 27 hand bones plus the distal radius and ulna are mapped separately. The actual palmar artwork places the thumb/radius on image-left, consistent with a right hand viewed from the palm; the source-page caption calls it left. Medulla follows the depicted anatomy and labels the view right palmar. The pisiform overlays the triquetrum. Source artwork is a teaching illustration and does not resolve every bony landmark.

## Foot

`foot-source.svg` and `app/foot-art.json`: VonTasha, Mario modesto, Uwe Gille, HLHJ and Johnuniq. Ospied-en.svg. Licensed CC BY-SA 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ospied-en.svg

The adapted foot geometry in `app/foot-art.json` is also distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0. Changes: removed legend and numerals, separated 26 selectable structures, standardized the view, changed colors and added interactive highlights. This license applies to this artwork adaptation, not independently authored application code. Original creators do not endorse Medulla.

## Thigh and leg

`skeleton-source.svg` and `app/leg-art.json`: Mariana Ruiz Villarreal (LadyofHats). Public domain.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Human_skeleton_front_no-text_no-color.svg

Adapted the right femur, patella, tibia and fibula from the anterior skeleton. Preserved outlines and shading paths; isolated the limb and changed colors. The patella is drawn over the distal femur. The tibia is medial, fibula lateral.

## Anatomy checks and remaining scope

Names, counts and major relationships checked against:
- https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-2-bones-of-the-upper-limb
- https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-4-bones-of-the-lower-limb

The other 18 plates remain simplified original teaching schematics with region-specific reference links. Detailed landmarks and further views require additional illustration and subject-matter review before positioning this as a comprehensive medical atlas.
